# Adversarial Reviewer Critique — All 8 Papers

Generated by 8 independent hostile-reviewer passes (NeurIPS/ICML area-chair stance),
each instructed to find every flaw so they can be fixed before submission. Reviewers
read the papers and cross-checked claims against the raw `results/` files.

This document is the consolidated, de-duplicated punch list. Severity tags:
**CRITICAL** (invalidates a headline claim), **MAJOR** (undermines a section),
**MINOR** (presentation/repro).

---

## Cross-cutting issues (appear in most papers)

1. **CRITICAL — Internal numeric inconsistency / inverted metrics.** Same quantity
   reported with different values across sections, and in two cases the numbers are
   genuinely inverted (success vs accuracy):
   - **Paper 5**: the headline "margin AUROC as low as −0.246 (ALP)" is a *delta*
     (0.9598→0.7141) misreported as an AUROC *value*; a negative AUROC is near-impossible.
     Vanilla PGD reported as "94.5% adversarial accuracy (5.5% success)" but the raw
     H173 file labels 0.9450 as PGD *success* — vanilla is the least robust, not robust.
   - **Paper 7**: Table 1 (Vanilla PGD succ 2.9%, Anti 0.6%) contradicts Table 2
     (97.1%, 99.4%) for the same H158 source — a ~95pp success/accuracy inversion. The
     abstract's "PGD success rises from 2.9% to 0.6%" is self-negating.
   - **Papers 1,3,4,6**: 3–4 different "margin AUROC on Fashion-MNIST" values quoted
     across tables with no per-cell (n, eps, ASR, seed) annotation → reads as cherry-picking.

2. **CRITICAL/MAJOR — No CIs, single seed, on load-bearing results.** Headline effects
   (Paper 2 Table 7 excess +0.65; Paper 8 intervention +0.65pp clean / hurt 103→93;
   Paper 5 defense hierarchy; Paper 6 0.019 gaps) are single-run point estimates. Several
   are within ~1 SE of zero. Papers claim "3 seeds" / "10 seeds" but raw files show n=1.

3. **CRITICAL/MAJOR — Saturation / class-imbalance confounds AUROC.** Attack success
   rates of 0.96 (Papers 3,4) leave ~3–5% minority class; AUROC is then minority-dominated,
   high-variance, and confounded with ASR. The matched-ASR fix (already done for Papers 1,2)
   must be applied to Papers 3,4,5.

4. **MAJOR — Circularity / leakage in predictors:**
   - Paper 1: `min_eps` is essentially the label (distance-to-misclassification) → oracle, not predictor.
   - Paper 3: in-ball gradient norm re-measures the attack it predicts; the "22× gap" is a
     scale artifact (AUROC is scale-invariant, so a uniform shrink can't change it).
   - Paper 6 (H180): the vulnerability label is PGD success **on the trained CNN**, scored
     against random-net margins — measures correlation-with-trained-model, not the random
     net's own vulnerability; and the readout is *supervised*, so "input geometry alone" overclaims.
   - Paper 8 §4.4: "AT-hurt" ≈ low margin, predicted by margin → AUROC 0.935 is near-tautological.

5. **MAJOR — Gradient masking never ruled out.** Papers 3,5,7 evaluate only FGSM/PGD.
   ALP shows FGSM succ 0.0000 (textbook masking); Paper 7's "worsens robustness" may be
   single-step obfuscation. `h174_autoattack_labels.py` exists and should be used as the label.

6. **MAJOR — Missing directly-relevant prior art (novelty overstated):**
   - **Margin Consistency**, arXiv 2406.18451 — logit margin as per-sample non-robust-sample
     score (pre-empts Papers 1/5/6 headline).
   - **DyART**, arXiv 2302.03015 — margin-aware boundary control; boundary moves toward some
     points / away from others (= Paper 8 "double jeopardy" mechanism; Paper 8 "exclude" is a
     crude binary DyART).
   - **Simon-Gabriel et al.** — adversarial vulnerability scales with input dimension
     (Paper 6 random-features / input-geometry).
   - **Tramèr et al. 2017** (transferable adversarial subspaces), **Demontis et al. 2019**
     (transferability factors) — Paper 2 shared-vulnerability.
   - **Swayamdipta et al.** dataset cartography (confidence vs variability) — Paper 4.

7. **MAJOR — Overclaiming in titles/abstracts** beyond what within-norm, matched-ASR,
   single-dataset evidence supports (Papers 2,4,8 especially).

8. **MINOR — Reproducibility**: seeds/configs/hyperparameters per table not released;
   "patched svhn results" in git log implies post-hoc edits.

## Per-paper top-3 most damaging

**Paper 1 (Benchmark).** (a) Cross-dataset claim invalid: CIFAR/SVHN/Imagenette crushed to
28×28 grayscale; the one native-resolution ResNet run *reverses* the headline (grad-norm >
margin). (b) Headline pre-empted by uncited 2406.18451. (c) Flagship AutoAttack/margin AUROC
numbers internally inconsistent (0.9936 vs 0.9370 in same source file).

**Paper 2 (Structural).** (a) The surviving +0.65 excess rests on an unverified "Chance"
column with no stated derivation/per-attack ASRs — could be relabeled base rate. (b) Zero
uncertainty quantification on the load-bearing Table 7. (c) Single model/eps/seed with
selection freedom in the matched-ASR eps.

**Paper 3 (Attribution).** (a) ASR 0.96/0.10 class imbalance makes every AUROC minority-
dominated (real cause of the "instability"). (b) In-ball "22× gap = mechanism" is a scale
artifact decoupled from the rank-based AUROC collapse. (c) Labels & predictor share possibly-
masked PGD gradients on AT models → "collapse" may be a masking artifact. SmoothGrad ≈ plain
gradient within noise (null buried).

**Paper 4 (Dynamics).** (a) ΔAUROC=−0.003 "redundant" is statistically unsupported (within-
noise, unpaired, collinearity can lower held-out AUROC artifactually). (b) Title "orthogonal"
contradicted by AUM=0.896; survives only by relabeling AUM as not-a-dynamics-signal. (c) AUM
computed on the **eval** set, not training set → not the Pleiss quantity; plus 96.6% ASR
degenerate task.

**Paper 5 (Defense geometry).** (a) −0.246 "negative AUROC" headline is a misread delta. (b)
Vanilla PGD success inverted (5.5% vs true 94.5%), contradicting the paper's own Table 5. (c)
margin AUROC confounded with ASR base rate; no seeds/CIs → single-run hierarchy unsupported;
ALP FGSM=0 = gradient masking.

**Paper 6 (Random features).** (a) H180 label is the trained CNN's attack outcome → near-
circular, not the random net's own vulnerability. (b) Supervised readout → "architecture-free
input geometry" overclaims; result largely pixel linear-separability; missing label-free
k-NN/pixel-distance baseline. (c) Three inconsistent "trained CNN" AUROCs (0.938/0.943/0.972);
H180 run at eps=15/255 while rest of paper uses 8/255.

**Paper 7 (Confirmatory).** (a) Contradictory PGD numbers across Tables 1 & 2 (~95pp). (b)
"Worsens robustness" unverified vs gradient masking; no AutoAttack/transfer. (c) Abstract
garbled/self-negating ("rises from 2.9% to 0.6%"); claim may reverse under correction. Missing
label-smoothing & FAT baselines.

**Paper 8 (Double jeopardy).** (a) No seeds/CIs on the single-run intervention; +0.65pp ≈ 1 SE,
hurt 103→93 ≈ 1 Poisson SE → "exclude wins" indistinguishable from noise. (b) §4.4 AUROC 0.935
near-tautological (predict low margin from margin). (c) "Exclude works" ignores the robustness
cost (PGD-ASR rises) — it just weakens AT on the hard samples (a TRADES tradeoff point), not a
free lunch. Missing DyART.

## Recommended remediation priority

1. **Fix the two factual inversions now** (Paper 5 −0.246 + PGD success; Paper 7 Tables 1/2 +
   abstract) — these are unambiguous, embarrassing, and verifiable against result files.
2. **Add CIs / multi-seed** to load-bearing results (re-run with ≥5 seeds; bootstrap/DeLong).
3. **Re-run headline AUROCs at matched ASR** for Papers 3,4,5 (saturation fix).
4. **Use AutoAttack labels** (h174) for Papers 3,5,7 to rule out gradient masking.
5. **Add the missing prior art** and soften titles/abstracts to match within-norm/matched-ASR/
   single-dataset scope.
6. **Add non-circular baselines**: label-free pixel/k-NN distance (Paper 6); demote circular
   AUROCs (Papers 1 min_eps, 8 §4.4) and lead with the intervention/controls.
